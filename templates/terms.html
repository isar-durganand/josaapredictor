2. Scope of Educational Advisory Service

JoSAA College Predictor provides informational advisory calculation tools structured on official historical cutoff archives published by the Joint Seat Allocation Authority (JoSAA). The software enables engineering aspirants to evaluate past admissions distributions based on self-reported ranks and eligibility criteria.

Important Counseling Advisory
Estimations generated by this tool constitute probabilistic approximations based on 2025 archived outcomes. They do not constitute a contractual guarantee or official seat allotment. Actual cutoffs fluctuate annually due to exam scoring trends, seat matrix modifications, and applicant choice distributions. All final selections must be verified via the official government portal at josaa.nic.in.
