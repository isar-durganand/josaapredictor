2. Scope of Educational Advisory Service

JoSAA College Predictor provides informational advisory tools built on official, post-counseling cutoff datasets released by the Joint Seat Allocation Authority (JoSAA). The software enables aspirants to evaluate historical trends based on self-reported rank and reservation credentials.

Crucial Counseling Advisory
Predictions generated by this platform constitute probabilistic historical approximations. They do not constitute an official allotment or guarantee of seat allocation. Annual cutoffs fluctuate due to exam session shifts, seat matrix adjustments, and applicant distributions. Users must verify all final choices via the official JoSAA counseling system (josaa.nic.in).
