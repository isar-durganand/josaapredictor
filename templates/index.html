Frequently Asked Questions

Essential details about counseling data, accuracy, and quota mechanisms.

The predictions are calculated directly from official JoSAA 2025 opening and closing rank archives. While cutoff trends are remarkably stable year-over-year, factors like total applicants, new branches, or seat matrix changes can shift closing ranks by 2–5%. We recommend using the "Moderate" and "Safe" classifications for conservative planning.

If you select a reserved category (such as OBC-NCL, EWS, SC, or ST), JoSAA cutoffs are published as Category Ranks. Therefore, you must enter your Category Rank for accurate results. If you are applying under the General/OPEN category, enter your Common Rank List (CRL) rank.

In NITs, 50% of the seats are reserved for students who completed their Class 12 exam within the same state where the NIT is located (Home State Quota). The remaining 50% are open to candidates across the country (Other State Quota). In IITs, all seats are All India (AI) quota with no state reservations.
