Step-by-Step Workflow

How the Prediction Engine Works

From raw NTA rank to a ranked preference shortlist in seconds.

Phase 1

Rank Ingestion

Input your All India Common Rank List (CRL) for General seats or your specific Category Rank for OBC-NCL, EWS, SC, or ST seats.

Phase 2

Quota Matching

Specify Home State (HS) versus Other State (OS) status, along with gender preferences to account for female supernumerary seat reserves.

Phase 3

Threshold Evaluation

The algorithm evaluates your rank against 72,000+ official closing ranks and calculates safety buffers (Safe, Moderate, Risky).

Phase 4

Preference Export

Download the calibrated match report as a CSV file to structure your official JoSAA choice-filling portal entries systematically.

Data Integrity & Disclaimers

All cutoff data utilized in this application is derived directly from verified Joint Seat Allocation Authority (JoSAA) post-counseling archives for the year 2025. We do not manufacture or alter official closing ranks.

Disclaimer: While historical cutoff trends are a reliable indicator of admissions, actual closing ranks for JoSAA 2026 may fluctuate based on seat matrix revisions, candidate choice distribution, and annual exam difficulty. This tool is intended for advisory and planning purposes.
