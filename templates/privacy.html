1. Overview

Welcome to JoSAA College Predictor ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to safeguarding candidate confidentiality and personal data. This privacy statement explains how information is processed when using our prediction tools and services across josaacollegepredictor.vercel.app.

2. Information Collection

2.1 Prediction Inputs

When utilizing the prediction engine, you input non-identifiable parameters including rank, category (e.g. OBC, EWS, SC, ST), quota preference (AI, HS, OS), and gender. These queries are evaluated dynamically in-memory and are not sold or persisted to tracking databases.

2.2 Technical & Analytics Metadata

Standard browser metadata (user agent, device class, latency, error logs) is collected automatically to optimize serverless performance, load times, and responsive display rendering.

3. Purpose of Processing

  • Delivering real-time admission probability scores across 128 premier colleges.
  • Maintaining serverless API reliability and system security.
  • Resolving technical bug reports submitted via the contact desk.
  • Ensuring compliance with web security standards and DDoS prevention.

4. Data Security

We implement modern HTTPS transport encryption, strict Content Security Policies, and serverless sandboxing to protect communications. Ranks entered into the prediction form are transiently evaluated in memory and discarded upon completion of your browser session.

5. Contact Information

For inquiries regarding this privacy statement, please contact the administrative desk at isardurganand@gmail.com or utilize our contact form.
