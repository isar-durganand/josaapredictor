Qualifying vs Admission

Scoring above the qualifying cutoff confirms your eligibility to appear for JEE Advanced. Actual admission into NITs, IIITs, or GFTIs requires meeting the institute-specific closing rank for your branch during JoSAA rounds.

Reservation Verification

Candidates claiming OBC-NCL or EWS reservations must have valid certificates issued on or after April 1 of the counseling year, adhering strictly to the central format prescribed by JoSAA.
